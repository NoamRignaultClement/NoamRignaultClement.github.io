Little Things

written, directed, shot, & edited by Noam Rignault Clement

Short Film. 6 min.

Taking his mother’s advice to enjoy the little things in life, a young boy in the charming French countryside embarks with a stolen garden gnome on an inquisitive quest to find just that.

THE DIRECTOR

Noam Rignault Clement

director | writer | cinematographer | editor | actor

Noam Rignault Clement is an 18-year-old French-American filmmaker and actor attending UC Berkeley. His narrative work, which explores the complexities of childhood, growing up, neurodivergence, and multiculturalism, has received multiple awards and nominations.

His latest short film, “Little Things” (2025), which he wrote, directed, filmed, and edited, is being screened at the 2026 San Francisco International Film Festival and was presented at the 2026 National Film Festival for Talented Youth festival (NFFTY).

Noam is currently in pre-production for his next short film “it ends with snails,” which touches on social justice and untangling interconnected lives through a childhood lens. He is actively seeking partners to bring his vision to life.
